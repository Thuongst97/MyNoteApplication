## PremiumNote 1.1.10 for Windows

Download **PremiumNote-Setup-1.1.10.exe** below (under *Assets*) and run it. Installing over an earlier version keeps all your notes.

> **First run on Windows:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### Office-style text color and fill
Select text on a page or a slide to use the new **Font color** and **Fill color** buttons:
- **Theme Colors** with lighter and darker shades, **Standard Colors** and **Recent Colors**.
- **Automatic** or **No Fill** to go back to the default.
- **More Colors…** opens the full color picker, and **Eyedropper** picks a color from anywhere on screen.
- Click the button itself to apply the last color again.

### Text alignment
- **Align left, Center, Align right and Justify** for paragraphs and headings, on pages and slides.
- Shortcuts: **Ctrl+Shift+E** (center), **Ctrl+Shift+R** (right), **Ctrl+Shift+J** (justify). Press the same shortcut again to go back to left.
