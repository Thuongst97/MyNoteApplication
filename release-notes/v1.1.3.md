## PremiumNote 1.1.3 for Windows

Download **PremiumNote-Setup-1.1.3.exe** below (under *Assets*) and run it. Installing over an earlier version keeps all your notes.

> **First run on Windows:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### New: Presentations
A new page type next to pages and whiteboards: **New presentation** in the sidebar, the new-tab page, or a page's menu.
- **Slides:** thumbnails on the left (add, duplicate, delete, drag to reorder); the current 16:9 slide in the middle, edited directly with every block type. Layouts (title, title & content, blank) and slide backgrounds, including dark ones.
- **Move anything, like PowerPoint:** each slide has its main text area plus boxes you place anywhere. Drag an object's edge or grip to move it (it snaps to the center, margins and other objects; hold Alt to place freely), resize it with its handles, and nudge it with the arrow keys.
  - **Insert** adds a text box, chart, table, image, code, diagram, drawing, mind map, equation, callout or HTML as its own box.
  - **Detach block** moves a block that's already in the text (for example a chart) into its own box.
  - Bring forward, send backward, duplicate, delete.
- **Animations:** slide transitions (fade, push, wipe, zoom) and click-by-click animations for blocks and boxes (appear, fade, fly in, zoom). Lists can appear one item at a time.
- **Draw:** pen, highlighter and eraser on slides.
- **Present:** full screen. → / Space / click moves forward, ← moves back, Esc exits; F5 starts from the beginning, Shift+F5 from the current slide.
  - While presenting: pen, highlighter, eraser and **laser pointer** (Ctrl+P / I / E / L), B to black the screen.
  - At the end you can keep or discard what you drew.
- Diagrams, charts and code on a slide are edited in a full-size window.

### New: Interactive HTML
- `/Interactive HTML` (or **Run JavaScript** in an HTML block) runs buttons, calculators, small games and demos.
- **Sandboxed:** scripts have no access to your notes or the app, and no internet unless you allow it for that block.
- Scripts from imported or pasted pages wait until you click **Run**.

### Tables
- Drag a column's right border to change its width, and a row's bottom border to change its height. Double-click a row border for automatic height.

### Charts
New **Format** tab (Edit → Format), like Excel:
- **Legend** on top, bottom, left, right, or hidden.
- **Data labels**, placed outside the end, inside the end, in the center, or at the base. Pies can show percent, value or both.
- **Number format:** number, compact (1.2K), percent, or currency ($ € £ ¥ ₫ ₩ ₹), with optional fixed decimals.
- **Axis titles**, gridlines on/off, stacked columns and bars, smooth lines, and three sizes.
