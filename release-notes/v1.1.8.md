## PremiumNote 1.1.8 for Windows

Download **PremiumNote-Setup-1.1.8.exe** below (under *Assets*) and run it. Installing over an earlier version keeps all your notes.

> **First run on Windows:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### Premium presentation themes
A new presentation opens a theme gallery. The **Theme** button changes the theme later and keeps your slides.
- **Obsidian:** keynote dark, with an electric light trail.
- **Graphite & Champagne:** luxury automotive, gold hairlines and an elegant serif.
- **Platinum:** premium light, cobalt accent, frosted cards.
- **Electric Night:** EV and digital cluster, with cyan headlight trails.
- **Carbon:** engineering and motorsport, carbon weave and signal red.
- **Aurora Glass:** in-car screens and Android Automotive, with frosted glass.
- **Neural (new):** for AI topics, with an iridescent glow and a neural network.

Each theme comes with a starter deck: title, agenda, key-number cards, chart, and closing. Themes style the fonts, colours, cards, agenda lists, eyebrow labels (Heading 4) and title artwork. Charts and diagrams follow the slide's light or dark look.

### Presentation utilities
- **Footer:** slide numbers ("03 / 12"), footer text and date, optionally hidden on title slides.
- **Hide slide:** hidden slides are skipped when presenting.
- **Align** a box or the text area to the slide: left, center, right, top, middle or bottom.
- **Speaker notes** under each slide.
- Area charts now fade softly to transparent.
