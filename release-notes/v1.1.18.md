## PremiumNote 1.1.18 for Windows

Download **PremiumNote-Setup-1.1.18.exe** below (under *Assets*) and run it. Installing over an earlier version keeps all your notes.

> **First run on Windows:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### Sidebar on the right
You can now put the sidebar on either side of the window:
- **Drag it:** press on the **My Notes** title and drag it toward the other side. A blue outline shows where it will go.
- **Or click** the panel icon next to « in the sidebar header (**Move sidebar to the right / left**).

On the right, it resizes from its left edge and collapses toward the right. The window buttons stay in the window's corners. Your choice is remembered.

### Fixes
- After you drag a page to a new place in the sidebar, the page list no longer stays tinted in two colours.
