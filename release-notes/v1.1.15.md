## PremiumNote 1.1.15 for Windows

Download **PremiumNote-Setup-1.1.15.exe** below (under *Assets*) and run it. Installing over an earlier version keeps all your notes.

> **First run on Windows:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### App themes
Click the new **palette** button at the bottom of the sidebar, next to Dark mode, to choose a theme for the whole app.
- **Light:** Classic Light, **Pure White**, Paper, Sand, Mint, Sky, Lavender, Rose.
- **Dark:** Classic Dark, Midnight, Arctic Night, Graphite, Forest.
- The app remembers one light theme and one dark theme. **Dark mode / Light mode** (Ctrl+Shift+L) switches between them.

### Light presentation themes
Five new light themes, each with its own title artwork and starter deck: **Ivory Editorial**, **Glacier**, **Studio White**, **Sage & Stone** and **Lumen**. The theme gallery now has an **All / Light / Dark** filter.

### PowerPoint-style shapes in drawings and whiteboards
- New rectangles, ellipses and diamonds are filled with a soft blue. Change the colour, or remove it, with **Background**. Pen strokes stay unfilled.
- Select a shape and just start typing: the text goes inside the shape. No Enter or double-click needed.

### Fixes
- **Large diagrams are no longer cropped** when you copy or export them as an image. Pictures now always contain the whole diagram, even from the zoomed full-window view.
- Mermaid diagrams with line breaks in their labels export correctly, and saved SVG files open in other programs.
