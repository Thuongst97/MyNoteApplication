## PremiumNote 1.1.14 for Windows

Download **PremiumNote-Setup-1.1.14.exe** below (under *Assets*) and run it. Installing over an earlier version keeps all your notes.

> **First run on Windows:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### Export diagrams from the expanded view
The full-window view of a **Mermaid** or **PlantUML** diagram (**Expand**) now has an **Export** button:
- **Copy as image:** a sharp PNG, ready to paste into Jira, Teams or mail.
- **Save as PNG or SVG…**

### Fixes
- Messages such as "Diagram copied as an image" now show on top of the expanded view instead of behind it.
